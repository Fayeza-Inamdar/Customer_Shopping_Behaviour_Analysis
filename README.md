🛍️ Customer Behavior Analysis – Power BI Project
---
📌 Project Overview
--
This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior to uncover key insights that help businesses make data-driven decisions. The analysis identifies trends related to revenue, purchasing patterns, demographics, seasonality, product categories, subscription preferences, and customer distribution across regions.

The entire workflow demonstrates end-to-end analytics skills—data cleaning, transformation, visualization, and business interpretation—using Power BI.

(https://github.com/Fayeza-Inamdar/Customer_Shopping_Behaviour_Analysis/blob/main/Screenshot%202026-03-22%20134745.png)

🎯 Objective
--
To analyze customer behavior across demographic groups, product categories, and time periods in order to:
✅ Understand customer revenue contribution
✅ Identify top-performing product categories
✅ Analyze season-wise shopping patterns
✅ Study influence of gender, age group & subscription status
✅ Evaluate overall customer experience using review ratings

🔍 Key Insights & Metrics
--
📊 KPIs Displayed

1. Total Revenue: 233.08K+

2. Avg Previous Purchase Amt: $25.35

3. Avg Purchase Amount: $60

4. Total Customers: 3900+

5. Avg Review Rating: 3.75

📈 Major Findings
-----
1. Clothing drives the highest revenue, followed by Accessories.

2. Fall and Spring seasons contribute the most sales.

3. Male customers contribute significantly higher revenue.

4. Most purchases come from Adult & Middle-aged customers.

5. Only ~27% of customers have an active subscription, yet they still generate a major share of revenue.

6. Customer distribution varies across US states, highlighting regional demand.
